# Congratulations Summer

-   [April 6, 2016](https://www.middleton.school.nz/2016/04/06/)

Summer Rutherford (Y13) represented Canterbury in the U18 women’s category at the New Zealand Track & Field Champs held in Dunedin early in March.

She had a fantastic weekend, achieving 3 Silver medals: 100m hurdles; 300m hurdles and Canterbury 4x100m relay; 4th place in long jump and she was also a finalist in the 100m Sprint! In the photo Summer is number 273.
