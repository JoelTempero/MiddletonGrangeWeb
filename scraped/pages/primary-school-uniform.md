## Primary School - Girls Summer

### Term 1 and 4

**Jacket**

MGS Polar Fleece. 

Optional with summer uniform

**Jersey**

MGS V-necked jersey or cardigan

**Dress**

MGS Dress. Compulsory for all school and official functions

**Hosiery**

Plain white fold-over ankle socks. NOTE: Heel socks, or “no show” socks are not to be worn

**Shoes/Sandals**

Plain black leather school lace-up, black leather T-bar, or U-shoes with regulation socks. Black leather sandals with back straps without socks. (NOTE: Heel no higher than 4 cm). Suede shoes or sports shoes are not to be worn

**Sunhat**

MGS Sunhat

**Optional**

MGS culottes (dress fabric) worn with MGS green short sleeved polo shirt

## Primary School - Girls Winter

### Term 2 and 3

**Jacket**

MGS Polar Fleece

**Pinafore**

MGS Pinafore

**Blouse**

MGS white long sleeve blouse with MGS monogram

**Jersey**

MGS V-necked jersey or cardigan

**Hosiery**

Black plain rib tights or black knee length plain rib socks

**Shoes**

Plain black leather school lace-up shoes or black leather T-Bar or U-Bar shoes. (NOTE: Heel no higher than 4 cm). Suede shoes or sports shoes are not to be worn.

**Optional**

MGS Scarf, MGS Beanie, dark green or black gloves

## Primary School - Boys Summer

### Term 1 and 4

**Jacket**

MGS Polar Fleece

Optional with summer uniform

**Jersey**

MGS V-necked jersey

**Shorts**

Light grey drill shorts

**Top**

MGS Short sleeve polo

**Socks**

Pale grey ankle socks

**Shoes/Sandals**

Plain black leather school lace-up shoes or Black leather sandals with back straps without socks. Suede shoes, sports shoes or slip on shoes are not to be worn

**Sunhat**

MGS Sunhat

## Primary School - Boys Winter

### Term 2 and 3

**Jacket**

MGS Polar Fleece

**Jersey**

MGS V-necked jersey

**Shorts**

MGS charcoal grey winter shorts

**Top**

Long sleeve MGS polo

**Socks**

MGS long winter socks with red, green and gold band at the top

**Shoes**

Plain black leather school lace-up shoes. Suede shoes, sports shoes or slip on shoes are not to be worn

**Optional**

MGS Scarf, MGS Beanie, dark green or black gloves

## Physical Education

### Compulsory for all Year 3-6 students

**Shorts**

Regulation MGS shorts

**Shirt**

Regulation MGS short sleeved PE shirt

**Socks**

White ankle socks

**Shoes**

Gym shoes or sport shoes (non-marking for Gymnasium)

**Optional**

Plain black trackpants or leggings, MGS black sports sweatshirt

## Rainwear

Raincoat– plain dark colour, to be worn over uniform. These are not to be worn in the classroom.
