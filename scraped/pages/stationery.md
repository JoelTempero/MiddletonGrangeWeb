To make ‘Back to School’ stationery purchasing easier, all school stationery can be purchased online at [myschool.co.nz/mgs](http://www.myschool.co.nz/mgs). Select your child’s subjects in the correct Year Level column (or Year Pack if your child will be in Years 1-6) and your cart will be pre-populated. You can then review your order to add or delete as required before using the checkout – internet banking, credit cards or debit cards and ZIP are available for payment. You can also specify which week you would like your order to arrive by courier. Courier is free for orders over $70.00 (incl GST) or $6.90 if under that.

You are of course free to purchase from other suppliers, but this simple and efficient system also benefits your children as we receive 10% cash back, which is used to purchase school resources.
