## Senior College - Girls Summer

### Term 1 and 4

**Blazer**

MGS Senior College Blazer.  
Optional with summer uniform

**Jersey**

MGS V-necked jersey MGS cardigan

**Skirt**

MGS Senior College skirt – on the knee

**Skort**

MGS skort

**Blouse**

MGS white short-sleeve blouse with MGS monogram

**Hosiery**

Plain white ankle socks

**Shoes/Sandals**

Plain black leather school lace-up shoes or plain black leather U-bar or T-bar shoes. T-bar shoes may be worn without socks. (Note: heel no higher than 4cm) or plain black leather sandals with back straps without socks.

N.B. Canvas, sports, suede shoes and boots are not acceptable

**Optional**

MGS Sunhat

## Senior College - Boys Summer

### Term 1 and 4

**Blazer**

MGS Blazer.  
Optional with summer uniform

**Jersey**

MGS V-necked jersey

**Shorts**

Regulation MGS charcoal grey shorts

**Shirt**

MGS short sleeved white shirt with MGS monogram

**Socks**

MGS grey socks

**Shoes/Sandals**

Plain black dress leather shoes with socks or plain black leather sandals with back straps without socks. N.B. Canvas, sports, suede shoes and boots are not acceptable.

**Optional**

MGS Sunhat

**NB: Boys’ summer shirts, Year 7 – 13**  
In 2022, the Middleton Grange Board agreed to change the boys’, Year 7 – 13 summer shirts to white.  
This was a considered decision, fed into by staff, parents and students via the uniform committee. The boys’ white summer shirt will be phased in over time as stock of the green shirt diminishes. The green shirt will remain part of the summer uniform, with the white shirt becoming sole uniform option in 2028.
