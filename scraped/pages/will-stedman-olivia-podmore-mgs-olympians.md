# Will Stedman & Olivia Podmore – MGS Olympians

-   [August 12, 2016](https://www.middleton.school.nz/2016/08/12/)

**Olivia Podmore :: New Zealand Olympic Cycling Team :: Rio Olympic Games 2016**  
Middleton Grange alumni Olivia Podmore is representing New Zealand at the 2016 Olympic Games in Rio. 

Olivia will be competing in the Individual Sprint, Team Sprint and Kierin for the Track Cycling Team.

**Olympic Games Schedule:**  
Team Sprint—13/8 – 7.00am  
Keirin—14/8 – 1.00am  
Ind Sprint—15/8 – 7.00am  
**  
William Stedman :: New Zealand Paralympics Team :: Rio Paralympic Games 2016**  
Y11 Pupil William Stedman is representing New Zealand at the 2016 Paralympic Games in Rio. 

William will be competing in the Long Jump, 400m and 800m T36 events where he is ranked 3rd, 3rd and 4th in the world   
respectively.   
**  
Paralympic Schedule:**  
Long Jump—Sept 12 

400m—Sept 15/16  
800m—Sept 17
