# Covid Update 10 : Changes to events

-   [September 4, 2020](https://www.middleton.school.nz/2020/09/04/)

Dear Parents, Caregivers and Whanau

**COVID – 19 (Coronavirus) Update 10**

**4 September 2020**

The Prime Minister has announced today that the Government will keep New Zealand at  
Alert Level 2 and Auckland at Alert Level 2.5 for at least another week and a half, until 11.59pm on Wednesday, 16 September 2020.

Obviously, this will have an ongoing impact on our school and the events we had planned over the next few weeks.  Can you please note the following:

-   **Course Selection Evening  
    **The course selection evening for our current Y10-12 students scheduled for next Tuesday, 8 September 2020 is cancelled. Information has been emailed to parents about the course selection process for your child/ren.
-   **Music Showcase  
    **The Music Showcase on the evening of Wednesday, 9 September 2020 will proceed with a limit of 100 people.
-   **Careers Expo  
    **The Careers Expo at Horncastle Arena scheduled for Thursday, 10 September 2020 has been cancelled.
-   **Haka Competition  
    **The school Haka competition scheduled for week 9 has been postponed.

We will keep you updated if there are any further changes.  If you have any questions about anything, please contact us at [office@middleton.school.nz](mailto:office@middleton.school.nz)
