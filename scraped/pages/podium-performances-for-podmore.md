# Podium Performances for Podmore

-   [October 22, 2015](https://www.middleton.school.nz/2015/10/22/)

Olivia Podmore represented New Zealand at the World Junior Track Cycling Championships  in Kazakhstan in late August, achieving some truly remarkable results on the world stage.

Olivia won a Silver medal in the Team Sprints, a Bronze medal in the 500m Time Trial, finished 6th in the Keirin and 7th in the individual Sprint.

Congratulations, Olivia, on this outstanding achievement! You have made the school community very proud.
