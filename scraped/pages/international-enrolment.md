**Student Visas**

The Immigration New Zealand website gives all the information on [applying for a Student Visa.](http://www.immigration.govt.nz/migrant/stream/study/application/)

If you are coming to New Zealand without a parent, you can [apply online](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/secure/Login+Student.htm) at Immigration New Zealand.

[inted@middleton.school.nz](mailto:inted@middleton.school.nz)

 

 

### 4\. We answer

**After we receive your application:**

1.  We will write a letter of acceptance or refusal
2.  You pay your fees (see Section 5 – Payment)
3.  We issue an Offer of Place
4.  You apply for a student visa
5.  Visa approved – make travel arrangements
6.  Advise the school of arrival date – Big Welcome!

 

 

### 5\. Payment

**Domestic payments** i.e. within New Zealand can be paid by bank card (eftpos), bank cheque, cash or via electronic transfer.  Please contact our office if you need our banking details.

**International Payments**

The school partners with Convera, which allows students, customers and agents a platform to transfer fees and avoid costly charges from your bank or other transfer facilities. Pay online with options including bank transfer, credit or debit card or eWallet. Go to **Pay your fees with Convera**  [https://students.convera.com/middletongrangeschool#!/](https://students.convera.com/middletongrangeschool#!/) to begin the payment process.
