### From Our Principal

Mr Gregg Le Roux

![](../media/2025/10/Gregg-Le-Roux.jpg)

It is my privilege to warmly welcome you to Middleton Grange School. It would be our honour to partner with you in the education of your children.

We believe that a life of Christ-like character, personal excellence, and a heart to serve others, bring both personal fulfilment and glory to our God.

In 1963, God was moving in the hearts of our schools’ founders. They carried a burden for young people to know Christ, coupled with a desire for an excellent education. I’m delighted to share with you that this vision endures today, by God‘s grace, strength, presence and power.

We look forward to welcoming your family, to become part of our family.

Ma te Atua tātou e manaaki – God bless you
