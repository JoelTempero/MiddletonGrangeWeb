The University in Christchurch is called ‘[**University of Canterbury**](https://www.canterbury.ac.nz/)‘.

Here is some information on [University Pathways](https://www.ucic.ac.nz/) and on [NCEA Requirements](https://www.ucic.ac.nz/admissions-requirements) to attend their International College.

[Lincoln University](http://www.lincoln.ac.nz) is also very near Christchurch.

The other main New Zealand universities are Auckland University; Massey University in Palmerston North; Victoria University in Wellington and Otago University in Dunedin.
