**Purchasing – New & Second-hand**

Primary School, Middle School and Senior College uniform can only be purchased new from the school uniform shop.

All uniform items can be purchased second-hand from the school uniform shop, except shoes.

-   **Shoe Clinic** stock a range of approved school shoes and have on file the footwear styles for Middleton Grange. Every pair purchased at Shoe Clinic earns our school $10 cash so please ensure your purchase is linked to us.
-   **The Athletes Foot** also give us a donation each year to help purchase sports equipment for pupils. This is based on how much money is spent by Middleton families at their Riccarton store, so be sure to link your purchase to us.

### Selling

Second-hand items which meet the high uniform standards we require can be sold through the school uniform shop. These can be brought to the shop during opening hours. Please bring along a completed [2nd Hand Uniform Form](../media/2018/10/uniform-2ndhand-form.pdf).

**Here are some guidelines:**

1.  Blazers and Kilts must be dry-cleaned and all other items washed and ironed.
2.  No stains, holes, fraying or discolouration. Any repairs must be done to a high standard.
3.  You will need your bank account number.
4.  When the item is sold payment is made by internet banking, less 30% commission.
5.  Shop staff reserve the right to reject any garment that does not meet the uniform standard.
