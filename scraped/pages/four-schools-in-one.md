Middleton Grange School is a large co-educational Christian School with around 1400 students in Years 1-13. In order to ensure that all students feel a part of the school, Middleton Grange School has been set up as four schools in one. One [Principal](https://www.middleton.school.nz/principals-welcome/), Mr Mike Vannoort, oversees all aspects of the school, and each of the four schools has a Head of School who oversees the teaching and learning and the pastoral care of the students within their school.

One of the major advantages for Middleton Grange School students is that there is a seamless approach to the Curriculum, pastoral care and Special Character as they transition from Primary School to Middle School and from Middle School to Senior College.  International students are mainstreamed across Years 2 – 13.

A whole-school-identity is promoted through [Special Character aspects](https://www.middleton.school.nz/special-character/) such as our [motto, verse, hymn, prayer and haka](https://www.middleton.school.nz/motto-crest-verse-hymn-prayer-haka/).

### The four schools are:

1.  [Primary School, Years 1-6](https://www.middleton.school.nz/primary-school-years-1-6/), with approximately 300 students.
2.  [Middle School, Years 7-10](https://www.middleton.school.nz/middle-school-years-7-10/), with approximately 450 students.
3.  [Senior College, Years 11-13](https://www.middleton.school.nz/senior-college-years-11-13/), with over 550 students.
4.  [International College](https://www.middleton.school.nz/international/), with around 100 students in Years 1-13
