### Character

### Character

To bring glory to God by becoming more and more like Jesus.

*Supplement your faith with a generous provision of moral excellence, and moral excellence with knowledge, and knowledge with self-control, and self-control with patient endurance, and patient endurance with godliness, and godliness with brotherly affection, and brotherly affection with love for everyone.*

2 Peter 1:5b-8 (NLT)

### Excellence

### Excellence

To bring glory to God by making the very best use of the talents and abilities he has given us.

*Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord, not for human masters.*

Colossians 3: 23 (NIV)

### Service

### Service

To bring glory to God by giving of ourselves to benefit others.

*Do nothing out of selfish ambition or vain conceit. Rather, in humility value others above yourselves, not looking to your own interests but each of you to the interests of the others.*

Philippians 2: 3-7 (NIV)

### For the Glory of God

### For the Glory of God

Our desire is to honour and please God in every aspect of our lives, with a particular focus on character, excellence and service.

*So whether you eat or drink or whatever you do, do it all for the glory of God.*

1 Corinthians 10:31 (NIV)
