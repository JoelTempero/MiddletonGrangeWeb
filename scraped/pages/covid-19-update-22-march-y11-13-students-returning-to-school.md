# Covid-19 / 22 March – Y11-13 Students returning to school

-   [March 22, 2022](https://www.middleton.school.nz/2022/03/22/)

Dear Parents, Caregivers and Whanau,

**Re: Senior College (Year 11 – 13) students returning to school**

As a school we have been monitoring the situation with our Senior College students very closely during the last week.

It is now at a stage where it appears manageable to staff, with most of our Senior College students being able to engage with on-site learning at school from next week (week 9).

We believe it is important to have the Senior College students back at school before the upcoming school holidays and feel that we will be able to manage staff and student absences adequately during this time.  We know that many of our Senior College students manage their learning well at home on their own, but we also know that some do not and that they would prefer on-site face to face learning.  This is especially true for those who take practical subjects.

As a school we want to ensure we are supporting ALL students with their academic achievement, as well as their wellbeing and success as a young person in Christ.  It is essential that ALL Senior College students attend school on the days listed below, unless they are sick or isolating.

**This return to school for Year 11 – 13 students will occur in a staged approach as follows:**

**Week 9 (beginning Monday 28 March):**

Each Senior College Year level will return to school for **TWO days only** for on-site learning:

**Year 13:** Tuesday 29 March & Wednesday 30 March

**Year 12:** Wednesday 30 March & Thursday 31 March

**Year 11:** Thursday 31 March & Friday 1 April

During these days – school uniform is to be worn, normal timetable will operate, and masks are mandatory for students when indoors.

On the other THREE days of week 9, Senior College students will continue their remote learning at home via TEAMS with work set by their teachers, however there will be no live lessons during those 3 days.

**Week 10 (beginning Monday 4 April):**

Each Senior College Year level will return to school for **THREE days only** for on-site learning:

**Year 13:** Wednesday 6 April, Thursday 7 April & Friday 8 April

**Year 12:** Tuesday 5 April, Wednesday 6 April & Thursday 7 April

**Year 11:** Monday 4 April, Tuesday 5 April & Wednesday 6 April

During these days – school uniform is to be worn, normal timetable will operate, and masks are mandatory for students when indoors.

On the other TWO days of week 10, Senior College students will continue their remote learning at home via TEAMS with work set by their teachers, however there will be no live lessons during those 2 days.

**Week 11 (beginning Monday 11 April): Last week of Term 1**

All Year 11 – 13 students are at school for **FOUR days** (Mon – Thur).  Friday is Good Friday (Easter).

It is envisaged that school will finish at lunchtime on Thursday 14 April for ALL students, Years 1 – 13.

**Please note the following important notice from Mrs Dixon, our NZQA coordinator:**

If your Son/daughter has been ill with covid and needs an extension for an Internal Assessment then make sure you have notified the school about the covid illness through [covidpositive@middleton.school.nz](mailto:covidpositive@middleton.school.nz) and your son/daughter needs to apply to Mrs Dixon for the extension.

If your son/daughter has been ill with some other illness then you, the parent, needs to email Mrs Dixon stating that your son/daughter has been ill with the dates that the illness covered and either you or your son/daughter needs to apply for the extension.

A reminder:

**IF YOU, OR A MEMBER OF YOUR HOUSEHOLD TEST POSITIVE FOR COVID-19, PLEASE CONTINUE TO CONTACT MIDDLETON GRANGE SCHOOL ASAP (including students already working from home) AT:**  Email [covidpositive@middleton.school.nz](mailto:covidpositive@middleton.school.nz)  

Thank you for your continued support and prayers as we partner with you in educating your children.
