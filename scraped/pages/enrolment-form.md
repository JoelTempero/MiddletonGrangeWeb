### Prior to Submitting

**Please Note**  

1.  The school will make direct contact with the Church Minister/Pastor via the contact details you have provided in order to obtain a confidential Minister/Pastor reference. If you answered 'Other' or 'Do not attend' to the church questions this will not occur.
2.  Please note the school reserves the right to return any incomplete application forms. Incomplete forms will not be processed. We do not hold applications from year to year. If you are unsuccessful for the year you are applying for, you will need to resubmit an application for the following year. Incomplete forms will not be processed.
3.  Should an offer of placement be made we will be requesting additional information to complete the application.

Please contact the school if you have not received acknowledgement within 8 working days of submission. Thank you for your application.
