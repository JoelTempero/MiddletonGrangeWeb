## Alumni Profile

# Katie Jo Van den Bogaert

-   Leaving Year: [2020](https://www.middleton.school.nz/tag/2020/)

![](../media/2022/07/image0-e1658441200146.jpeg)

Katie has been in America for 8 months now, studying communications and business at Whitworth University in Spokane Washington.  She has just finished her first season as a Freshman on the woman’s basketball team.  She will be staying there over the Summer to continue working and training for the upcoming season.

“I am loving the people here and have found a great group of people to call my American family. I have been very blessed with everyone I have met and am looking forward to the next 3 years at this school”
