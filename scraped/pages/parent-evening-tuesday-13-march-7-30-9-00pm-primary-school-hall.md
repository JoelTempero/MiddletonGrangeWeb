# Parent Evening – Tuesday 13 March 7.30-9.00pm Primary School Hall

-   [February 28, 2018](https://www.middleton.school.nz/2018/02/28/)

**Acorns into Oaks of Righteousness.**

We are privileged to welcome Dr Michael Lindsay, President of Gordon College, USA to speak with parents at this Parent Evening.  This inspirational talk draws on scripture and a decade-long research project of 550 senior leaders to help parents understand how to embed the importance of faith and virtue as they prepare students to become global leaders for our pluralistic world. With data from the largest leadership study ever undertaken and insights from the Book of Isaiah, this engaging lecture will offer parents insights into the daily disciplines and major milestones that shape the hearts and minds of students today. The talk will also offer several practical approaches to parenting young people who are able to thrive as committed Christ-followers in an increasingly secular, complex environment. This will be a very special evening dedicated to helping us parent our children more effectively!
