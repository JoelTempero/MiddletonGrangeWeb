Tena Koutou Katoa!

Middleton Grange Primary School caters for 300 students from New Entrants to Year 6.  Our excellent reputation is based on ![](../media/2023/01/Christine-Buckley.jpeg)high academic standards, a caring, family atmosphere and a wide range of cultural and sporting opportunities.  Our staff are all committed to ensuring positive relationships are developed with parents and caregivers,  are committed to work with individual students and meet the students where they are at in their learning journey.  We offer this within a Christian environment where the values of Jesus are taught and modelled.

It is a privilege to lead the Primary School, along with an experienced staff of Primary Teachers. We are dedicated to laying foundations for our students to grow academically, socially, emotionally, and spiritually. Our dedicated staff nurture the strengths and abilities of your child, seeing them as children created in God’s image.

The strengths of our Primary School:

-   Committed, caring, and skilled team of teachers
-   Supportive parents and wider school community
-   Well-equipped and attractive facilities
-   Teaching and learning programmes that cater for a wide range of learning needs

We look forward to working along side you as parents and whanau, throughout your child’s Primary School years as they learn to use their Head, Hands and Heart to bring glory to God.

Nga Mihi Nui

**Christine Buckley**  
Deputy Principal – Primary School
