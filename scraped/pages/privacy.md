Our Privacy Policies and Procedures comply with the NZ Privacy Act 2020.

All information collected is for the purpose of student education and as outlined in our full Privacy Policy which can be found with other policies [https://www.middleton.school.nz/board/](https://www.middleton.school.nz/board/)

Personal information about students may be shared with the Ministry of Education (MoE), New Zealand Qualifications Authority (NZQA), Attendance Service, District Health Board (DHB) and Resource Teachers Learning and Behaviour (RTLB). Non-identifying statistical information may be shared with other relevant agencies to help with school planning and administration.

You have the right to ask for a copy of any personal information we hold about you, and to ask for it to be corrected if you think it is wrong. If you’d like to ask for a copy of your information, or to have it corrected, please contact us at [office@middleton.school.nz](mailto:office@middleton.school.nz)
