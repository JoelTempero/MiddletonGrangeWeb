# Board Triennial Elections

-   [June 13, 2022](https://www.middleton.school.nz/2022/06/13/)

**Middleton Grange School Board Elections**

Board elections are being held this year as part of the triennial cycle.  If you are thinking of serving the school community in this capacity as a Parent Representative, please feel free to talk to any of our existing board members about what the role involves and whether this may be something you could be involved in.  We desire to have parents who are representative of our community, who have a love for our Lord and who wish to use their gifts and talents to serve God and the school community.  If you would like to speak to one of our board members, please contact [office@middleton.school.nz](mailto:office@middleton.school.nz) to arrange this.

Please note the key dates and details below:

-   **Nominations Close                           Wednesday, 3 August / Week 2 Term 3**
-   **Election Day                                        Wednesday 7 September**
