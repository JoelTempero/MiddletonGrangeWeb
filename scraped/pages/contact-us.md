### Absences

To leave an absence message you can use the School App by clicking on Absentee button, phone using extension listed below or email [absences@middleton.school.nz](mailto:absences@middleton.school.nz)

### Phone Extensions

Ph +64 (03) 348 9826. During office hours please select from the following options:

-   To record an **absence or lateness** – press 1
-   Primary **reception** – press 2
-   Main reception and **enrolment** information – press 3
-   Principal’s EA – press 4
-   Fees and **accounts** – press 5
-   Student Services **(Y7-13)** – press 0

### International College

International College absences and contact information is different. [Details here.](https://www.middleton.school.nz/international-contact/)

### The school office consists of two main areas and three sub-areas:

-   **Reception** is for general inquiries and where you can pay accounts, receive information about enrolments, get directions or meet a staff member you have an appointment with.
-   **Student Services** is for any contact with students, for example sick bay, deliveries or messages to go to students or staff, return slips, absences, sign-in and sign-out tablet.
-   **International College Reception**
-   **Primary Admin**
-   **Middle School/Senior College Admin**
