# Elf the Musical Junior

-   [December 13, 2017](https://www.middleton.school.nz/2017/12/13/)

Rehearsals for Elf The Musical Junior were a lively and festive part of the Middleton Grange Performing Arts community this year. Middle school pupils’ hard work to polish songs and choreography created a sparkling performance on stage. 

Comments about the show have been a wonderful tribute to the team of performers.  We congratulate Keenan Aubrey who played the lead role of Buddy the Elf, strongly supported by an excellent ensemble and company. Thank you also to staff and crew for their enthusiasm, tuition and commitment, and to the wider school community for support of this festive event.
