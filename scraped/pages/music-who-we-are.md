## Vision Statement

We work to provide a seamless and accessible instrumental and vocal programme that is affordable and of the highest quality, connecting MGS students to the wider school music community and preparing them for co-curricular groups, and curriculum music.

Promote

Promoting music to all as a vehicle for worshiping and glorifying God.

Inspire

Inspiring strong performance groups across genres, in which students are trained to be effective in their craft.

Grow

Growing music in its many forms and styles through tuition and performance experience.

Encourage

Encouraging a culture of enjoyment within Music, as a desirable, achievable subject.

Engage

Engaging the musically talented students in our community and equipping them in their gifts.
