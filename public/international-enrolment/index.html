Student Visas

The Immigration New Zealand website gives all the information on applying for a Student Visa.

If you are coming to New Zealand without a parent, you can apply online at Immigration New Zealand.

 

 

2. Fill in

Contract of Enrolment

 

 

3. Send

Post or email completed Application Forms to:

Middleton Grange International College
30 Acacia Avenue
Christchurch 8041
New Zealand
inted@middleton.school.nz

 

 

4. We answer

After we receive your application:

  1. We will write a letter of acceptance or refusal
  2. You pay your fees (see Section 5 – Payment)
  3. We issue an Offer of Place
  4. You apply for a student visa
  5. Visa approved – make travel arrangements
  6. Advise the school of arrival date – Big Welcome!

 

 

5. Payment

Domestic payments i.e. within New Zealand can be paid by bank card (eftpos), bank cheque, cash or via electronic transfer.  Please contact our office if you need our banking details.

International Payments

The school partners with Convera, which allows students, customers and agents a platform to transfer fees and avoid costly charges from your bank or other transfer facilities. Pay online with options including bank transfer, credit or debit card or eWallet. Go to Pay your fees with Convera  https://students.convera.com/middletongrangeschool#!/ to begin the payment process.
