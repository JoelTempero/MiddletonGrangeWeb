Middleton Grange embraces the National Curriculum in all subject areas, and interprets these statements from a biblical perspective.

High academic standards are emphasised. Students are guided in setting goals and supported in working towards achieving them.

Primary School

All curriculum areas are taught by the classroom teacher. Where teachers have areas of expertise, an interchange system is set up to utilise their skills.

Senior College

Academic Colours are awarded in each subject to students who meet the criteria. Besides academic courses, we offer a wide variety of alternative pathways, including S.T.A.R courses, and work experience.

Effective time management and organisational skills – two vital elements of successful academic progress. To assist students in these areas, Senior College utilises the Success Planner programme utilising:

  • The partnership of parents, students and school
  • A Wall Planner for planning time and keeping track of progress
  • A structured study programme – delivered by the school
