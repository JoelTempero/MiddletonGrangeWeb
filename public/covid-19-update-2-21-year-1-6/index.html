Covid 19 Update 2-21 / Year 1-6

Kia ora parents and caregivers

From today we are operating under Alert Level 2 and the following measures will apply:

  • Your children can continue attending school and it is safe to do so.
  • Anyone who is unwell or showing symptoms of illness must stay home and should have a Covid-19 test. Continue self-isolating until you get the all-clear from the test.
  • We will be continuing to emphasise personal hygiene and hand washing at school. Children may wear masks if they wish.
  • We will maintain social distancing by separating classroom desks and spreading out seating areas. We will avoid large gatherings.
  • Please do not come onto the school grounds with your child unless this is absolutely essential. If you do need to enter the school, please sign in at Primary Admin or the main school office. Keep your visit brief and avoid close contact with others.
  • Wednesday’s assembly is cancelled.
  • Year 6 Trek-About on Tuesday will continue as planned.

Please keep calm and trust in the Lord. Our children will look to adults for guidance and reassurance so it’s important that we show sensible leaderhsip.

Regards
