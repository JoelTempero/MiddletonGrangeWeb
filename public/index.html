We want students who notice the new kid, put their hand up, and finish what they start. Who play hard, lose well, and laugh about it on the bus home. Who can sit in silence with a friend, and who know the One in whose light we see all of it.

iv. The Four Schools
i. ii. iii. iv.

Primary School

Years 1 – 6 · ~300 ākonga

Foundations take quiet, patient work. We teach reading like it matters, because it does, and we let children be children: puddles, friends' names, finding out what they are good at.

Walk through Primary School →

Middle School

Years 7 – 10 · ~450 ākonga

In the middle years the questions get harder and the answers get more interesting. We meet that with responsibility, room to fail, good friendships and a great deal of patience.

Walk through Middle School →

Senior College

Years 11 – 13 · ~550 ākonga

NCEA, leadership, service, university and trades. Our seniors leave at the end of Year 13 well-prepared and well-loved. Only one of those goes on a brochure.

Walk through Senior College →

International College

Years 1 – 13 · ~100 ākonga from 14 countries

For students from overseas: homestay families, ESOL, pastoral care, university pathways, and a tutor who will text your parents in their own time zone when you are sick. We make it a second home on purpose.

Char­acter. Excel­lence. Service. For the glory of God.

i.

Char­acter

Becoming more like Jesus, to the glory of God. How we treat each other shows who we are becoming.

“Supplement your faith with moral excellence…” · 2 Peter 1:5–8
ii.

Excel­lence

The best use of the talents we have been given: at our desks, on the field, on the stage, in the workshop.

“Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart…” · Colossians 3:23
iii.

Ser­vice

Giving ourselves for the good of others. We look outward and put others first.

“In humility value others above yourselves…” · Philippians 2:3
iv.

For God's glory

Whatever we do, whether we eat or drink or learn or play, we do it for the glory of God.

“…do it all for the glory of God.” · 1 Corinthians 10:31
