Donations

Middleton Grange School continues in its commitment to training young people in servanthood to transform New Zealand’s culture and to give students the best Christian education possible.  The teachers, support staff, and the community take this commitment seriously and work together to deliver the Christian education and encouragement that will strengthen students’ relationships with God and with each other.

Seeds are being planted that, with the proper nourishment, will continue to grow, and we want to invite you to join with us in this important task.

Through the Alumni network, we are reaching toward a challenging but exciting vision: to advance the work and cause of Christian education in New Zealand.  We hope to do this by training and equipping tomorrow’s Christian leaders here at Middleton Grange.

Please email us at alumni@middleton.school.nz to obtain our bank details should you want to make a donation or contribute to one of our fundraisers.
