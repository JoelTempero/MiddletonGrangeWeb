Lip Sync

What an amazing and entertaining evening of music and fun. The competition was fierce and plenty of vocal support from the audience added to the atmosphere. 

Scott House impressed the judges the most with their music, crazy costumes, strong male lead and great supporting performances. Wilson came in a close second with the travel theme, shared spotlight and great costumes. Shackleton did well to come third, in spite of small numbers, but they cleverly linked this sense of abandonment to their theme and song choices. Bowen’s strength was their humour and involvement by all the participants, but the lip syncing was not quite up to lifting them out of 4th place. It was an enjoyable evening for both performers and audience.
