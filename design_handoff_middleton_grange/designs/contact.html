Getting here

A short way in.

By car

Off Yaldhurst Rd.

Visitor parking via the Acacia Avenue gate. Drop-off circle by reception. Five minutes from Westfield Riccarton, fifteen from the central city.

By bus

Metro 60 & 80.

Routes 60 and 80 stop on Yaldhurst Road. School-run buses pick up around greater Christchurch each morning and evening.

By bike

Covered racks.

Secure bike racks at the main entrance and at each school. The Hagley Park cycle network runs to within five minutes of campus.
