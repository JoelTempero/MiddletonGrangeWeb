Curriculum & subjects

Three strands, thirteen years.

The New Zealand Curriculum runs the length of the school. We teach to it — and beyond it — with a Christian worldview woven through every lesson. Class sizes are kept small enough that every child is known and stretched.

Years 1 – 6 · Primary

Foundations (literacy & numeracy)

The early years are about reading well, writing well, and the love of both. Structured phonics, hands-on maths, inquiry, and rich storytelling. Christian Studies is daily.

  • Literacy & structured phonics
  • Mathematics & problem-solving
  • Science, Technology & STEM
  • Visual arts, drama, music
  • Physical education
  • Christian studies & chapel
Years 7 – 10 · Middle

Specialists (stretch & bridge)

Subject specialists join the homeroom teacher. Students choose options. Independence, study skills, time management and outdoor education all develop in earnest.

  • English, Mathematics, Sciences
  • Social sciences & languages
  • Technology & digital technology
  • Visual arts, music, drama, dance
  • PE & outdoor education
  • Christian studies & service
Years 11 – 13 · Senior

NCEA (pathways & depth)

Level 1, 2 and 3 NCEA across all learning areas. Scholarship classes for top performers. Vocational and trades pathways through Gateway. Course handbooks at the foot of this page.

  • English, Mathematics, three Sciences
  • History, Geography, Economics
  • Te Reo Māori, Spanish, Japanese
  • Visual arts, music, drama, media
  • PE, health, outdoor education
  • Religious studies / scholarship

Performing arts · 11 strands

The stage.

Performing arts at Middleton runs from Year 4 right through to leaving Year 13 with a Bachelor of Music portfolio. Eleven strands, one theatre, one radio station, one ridiculously good annual production. Every student is welcome — beginner or grade eight.

Strand i.

Middleton Music Academy

The flagship music programme — individual tuition, ensembles, examinations, performance pathways.

Year-round · all year levels Strand ii.

Music Tuition

One-to-one instrumental and vocal lessons during the school day. Twelve instruments offered.

Termly enrolment Strand iii.

Instrument Hire

Hire an instrument for the year. We have flutes, clarinets, trumpets, violins, cellos, guitars.

Annual hire Strand iv.

Performance Groups

Concert band, jazz band, orchestra, chamber groups, choirs and vocal ensembles.

Auditioned · weekly Strand v.

School Production

One large-scale production a year at The Grange Theatre. Year 10 – 13 cast, Year 7 – 13 crew. Three nights, usually sold out.

Term 2 — opening 28 May Strand vi.

Middleton Dance Academy

Ballet, contemporary, jazz, hip-hop. RAD examinations, choreography, and the annual showcase.

Year-round Strand vii.

Drama

NCEA drama, Shakespeare Globe Centre auditions, Sheilah Winn festival, student-directed productions.

Year-round · Years 7 – 13 Strand viii.

Kapa Haka

Our kapa haka roopu performs at school occasions, regional festivals and at events for the wider community.

Year-round · open Strand ix.

Pasifika

Our Pasifika group celebrates the cultures of our Pacific students, including dance, music and language.

Year-round · open Strand x.

Tech Crew

The students who make every production happen — lighting, sound, video, set, stage management. Trained on professional equipment.

By application Strand xi.

The Fridge Radio · 88.3 FM

Christchurch's only student-run FM radio station. Live shows, music, interviews. Heard all the way to Rangiora.

Year-round · Y9 – 13
Te Ohu Kahika · STEM centre

A laboratory for the curious.

Te Ohu Kahika is our standalone STEM and innovation centre. It runs Saturday lab days for Year 9 and 10 students, current workshops in robotics and biotech, sponsorship-funded programmes and partnerships with the University of Canterbury and local industry.

Students who do not know yet that they are scientists, mathematicians or engineers come here and find out. Students who do know come here to go deeper, faster.

Support, care & what's next

Around the classroom.

Pastoral Care

Each student has a tutor teacher who is their first point of contact, a Head of School, and access to school counsellors and the chaplaincy team.

Heads of School · Chaplain

Learning Support

Our team works with students who need additional academic, social or emotional support. Individualised plans, sometimes one-on-one, always confidential.

Learning Support Coordinator

Careers & Gateway

Career counselling from Year 9. University and trades guidance for seniors. Gateway placements for hands-on workplace experience.

careers.gateway@…

Service & Leadership

Senior leadership: prefects, House captains, peer support, service trips (Vanuatu, local). Leadership is taught, not assumed.

Service Coordinator

BYOD

Year 7 and up bring a device. Recommended specs and a subsidised purchasing portal through Cyclone Computers. Wi-Fi blanket-covered campus.

Remote Learning

A full remote learning programme exists for when students or whole schools need it — built and refined through COVID, kept in working order.

Overseas Trips

Service trips to Vanuatu in the July holidays. Sports tours. Music tours. Languages exchanges. The world is on the curriculum.
