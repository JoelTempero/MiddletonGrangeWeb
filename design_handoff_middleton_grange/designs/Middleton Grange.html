A letter, in brief

We are a school of about fourteen hundred young people, on one campus, with one verse.

Middleton Grange is small enough to know a child by name and big enough to hold them — through their first nervous walk into a Year 1 classroom, through Year 9's first NCEA grade, through opening night of the senior production. We are Christian, state-integrated, sixty-two years old this year, and very much rooted in this corner of Ōtautahi.
— Mr Gregg Le Roux, Principal

We want our students to be the kind of people who notice — who see the new kid, who put their hand up, who write a thank-you note, who finish what they began. Who play to win, lose with grace, and laugh about it on the bus home. Who can sit in silence with a friend, and who know the One in whose light we see all of it.

The four words on the gate

Char­acter. Excel­lence.
Service. For the
glory of God.

i.

Char­acter

To bring glory to God by becoming more like Jesus. The way we treat each other reflects who we are becoming.

“Supplement your faith with moral excellence…” — 2 Peter 1:5–8
ii.

Excel­lence

The very best use of the talents we have been given — at our desks, on the field, on the stage, in the workshop.

“Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart…” — Colossians 3:23
iii.

Ser­vice

Giving of ourselves for the good of others. Looking outward. Valuing others above ourselves.

“In humility value others above yourselves…” — Philippians 2:3
iv.

For God's glory

Whatever we do — eat, drink, learn, play — we do it all for the glory of God. Purpose woven through.

“…do it all for the glory of God.” — 1 Corinthians 10:31
Four Houses, named below the ice

The school looks south.

Three of our four Houses are named for the men who set out from Lyttelton bound for Antarctica — Scott, Shackleton and Wilson. The fourth, Bowen, remembers the family on whose paddocks the school was founded.
