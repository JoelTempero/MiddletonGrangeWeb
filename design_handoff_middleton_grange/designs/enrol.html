Why choose us

A few reasons families say yes.

i.

Christ at the centre.

Our Christian worldview is the foundation, not an add-on. It shapes how we teach, how we coach, how we run the canteen and how we handle the hard days.

ii.

Every student known by name.

Our pastoral system is built so no student falls through the cracks. Tutor teachers know their tutor group well. Heads of School know the parents.

iii.

Strong NCEA results.

Our seniors consistently exceed national NCEA averages. University Entrance, scholarships and trades pathways are well-trodden — and well-supported.

iv.

The whole child.

Sports, arts, service, leadership, faith. We develop the whole person — not just the academic record. Most of our students will play in a band, on a team, and in a school production at some point.

v.

One campus, one community.

Year 1 to Year 13 on the same Upper Riccarton site means younger and older students mix daily, siblings travel together, and the school community is genuinely one.

Frequently asked

A few honest answers.

Tap any question to read the answer. If yours isn't here, write to enrolments@middleton.school.nz — we read every email.

01.

Do we need to be a Christian family to enrol?

No. We welcome families of all backgrounds. We do, however, ask that all families understand and support our Special Character — that the Bible shapes everything we do, that students will attend chapel and Christian studies, and that we will pray.

+
02.

What does "state-integrated" actually mean?

It means we are part of the state schooling system — funded by the Ministry of Education like any other state school — but we have a Proprietor (the Christian Schools' Trust) who safeguards our Special Character. We charge attendance dues to support the Proprietor's costs.

+
03.

What if we're out of zone?

Out-of-zone applications go into a ballot run each spring. Siblings of current students are given priority, then Special Character preference, then general ballot. Applications must be received by 15 August for the following year.

+
04.

Do you offer learning support?

Yes. We have a Learning Support team across all four schools, working with students who need additional academic, social or emotional support. Please share any documentation at application — it helps us put the right support in place from day one.

+
05.

What about international students?

Our International College welcomes students from around the world. We arrange homestay accommodation, ESOL support, university pathways and full pastoral care. Contact inted@middleton.school.nz for the international fee schedule.

+
06.

Are fees negotiable?

Attendance dues are set by the Proprietor and are payable; the Special Character Donation is voluntary and tax-deductible. If cost is a barrier to enrolment, please contact the office in confidence — we have arrangements for families experiencing genuine hardship.
